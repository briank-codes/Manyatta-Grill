Our Story

Where Culture Meets the Jiko

Manyatta Grill was born from a love of bold Kenyan flavours and community dining. Every dish is prepared with care using traditional grilling methods and locally sourced ingredients.

Our menu blends heritage and warmth with a modern dining experience. Whether you join us for a family meal or a celebration, we make sure every visit feels special.

Fresh cuts and seasonal produce sourced from trusted local farmers.

🌿 Traditional Recipes

Classic Kenyan favourites with a modern presentation.

A warm place for family dinners, celebrations, and community gatherings.
